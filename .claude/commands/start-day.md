Get the person moving in under three minutes. Short replies, one question at a
time if anything needs asking.

## 1. Gather, do not ask

```bash
./life status
```

Read the top of `current.md`, all of `queue.md`, and the "How my brain works"
and "Fixed commitments" sections of `context.md`. Check the ritual notes in
the "About the person" section of `AGENTS.md`.

## 2. One question: energy

Ask: "Energy today: low, ok, or high?" (skip if they already said).

## 3. Propose Now

- low: 1 item, or one basic-care task. Say that counts as a full day.
- ok: 2 items.
- high: 3 items, hardest one in their peak focus window.

Draw from yesterday's "Stopped at", then Now, then Next, then Waiting-on
items that may have unblocked. Respect today's fixed commitments. Show the
proposal as a numbered list with a rough time for each.

Ask "Go with this?" and adjust once if needed.

## 4. Write it

Update the Now section of `queue.md` (move displaced items back to Next;
never delete). Do not touch `current.md` unless the topic changed.

## 5. First step

End with ONE action for item 1 that takes under two minutes, e.g. "Open the
tax folder and find last year's return." Nothing else after it.
